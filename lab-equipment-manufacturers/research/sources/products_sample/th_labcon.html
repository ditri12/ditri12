Locbon also provides a range of labconco freeze dryers, suitable for freeze-drying or lyophilization applications. You can be confident with the preservation of structure and properties. In addition to this, Labcon also provides an array of well plates and centrifuge tubes. We boast a range of laboratory equipment and accessories at Thomas Scientific to meet laboratory needs and benefit from lab con products to assist with storing, holding, and transferring applications in and around the lab.
