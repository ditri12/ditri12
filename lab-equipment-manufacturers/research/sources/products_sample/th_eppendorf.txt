Eppendorf Lab Equipment Supplier
Eppendorf is a leading life science company that develops and sells instruments, consumables, and services for liquid, sample and cell handling in laboratories worldwide. Its product range includes pipettes and automated pipetting systems, dispensers,
centrifuges
,
mixers
