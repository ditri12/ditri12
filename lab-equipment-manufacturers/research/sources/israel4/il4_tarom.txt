JOIN TAROM'S VISION

Founded in 1989, Tarom Applied Technologies Ltd. is one of Israel’s leading distributors of cutting-edge solutions for Clinical Diagnostics, Life Science Research and Development, Bio- and Chemical industries, as well as a wide range of general industries.
Our long-standing scientific and commercial expertise and commitment to quality have positioned us at the forefront of technological distribution in Israel. This expertise, combined with our personalized service and foundation of trust, has enabled us to forge strong, long-term partnerships with leading technology innovators and healthcare providers.
