Our Partners

A Heritage of Enduring Alliances

Since the early 1960s, we have cultivated strong and lasting relationships with global leaders in healthcare and life sciences. Many of these collaborations have grown over decades, evolving from distribution agreements into deep strategic alliances that continue to shape Israel’s healthcare and scientific landscape.

We believe true partnerships are founded on trust, shared values, and mutual success. We select our business partners with care, focusing on a shared commitment to excellence to ensure our clients receive the highest quality and value.

Our Partnership Philosophy

Our Partnership Philosophy

Trust and Professionalism

We establish long-term relationships built on trust, transparency and collaboration. For us, partnerships are not merely commercial agreements, but close working relationships dedicated to achieving excellence. This commitment extends far beyond initial product delivery, with comprehensive, 360-degree lifecycle support designed to ensure the optimal implementation of our solutions and the sustained success of our clients. As we proudly affirm:
“Service is Our Science”.

Driving Innovation

We are committed to bringing the forefront of global technology to the Israeli market. To achieve our goals, we partner with pioneers and leaders in their respective fields. By fostering mutual learning and a continuous exchange of knowledge, we ensure that both our partners and clients benefit from the most impactful industry advancements.

A Shared Vision

Our partners are integral to our mission of advancing healthcare and scientific progress in Israel. Through long-term collaboration, we jointly implement groundbreaking technologies that improve lives and raise professional standards across the medical and life science sectors. Together, we create enduring and meaningful change.
