Life Science Division

Eldan’s Life Science Division is your trusted partner for a comprehensive range of scientific solutions. We are devoted to bringing state-of-the-art instruments, consumables, reagents, kits, and services from top global manufacturers to the Israeli market. By cultivating robust partnerships, we are able to provide end-to-end solutions for a diverse array of customers, including universities and research centers, biotech, pharma and chemical industries, hospitals, medical centers and diagnostics labs, and governmental institutions.

We support clinical lab, research and industry progress, with expertise in a wide range of fields such as chemical analysis and spectroscopy, genomics and molecular biology, clinical diagnostics and scientific solutions. Our complete solution range extends from import, regulatory approval, sales and distribution to service, technical and application support – ensuring continous and expert service.

Automated Liquid Handling

Eldan’s Automated Liquid Handling portfolio provides advanced, space-saving, and flexible solutions that enable simplified sample preparation with consistent high accuracy. We offer powerful open liquid handling platforms, from global leaders like
Agilent
, which can be tailored to serve a broad range of application needs, from genomics and NGS to proteins and metabolites.

Learn More about Automated Liquid Handling

Blood Banking

Eldan’s Blood Banking portfolio offers the broadest range of high-quality solutions for blood banks, labs, and transfusion centers across Israel. We partner with global leader
Werfen

(Immucor) to provide cutting-edge technologies and comprehensive workflows.

Learn More about Blood Banking

Chromatography & Mass Spectrometry

Eldan’s Chromatography & Mass Spectrometry portfolio has a wide range of analytical solutions across Israel. For over four decades, we have proudly and represented
Agilent
, a global leader in the field, alongside other top-tier manufacturers.

Learn More about Chromatography & Mass Spectrometry

Consumables & Supplies

Eldan’s Consumables & Supplies portfolio is a trusted source for a range of essential products for analytical labs. These include chromatography columns, instrument supplies, chemical standards, sample preparation products, syringes, needles, and vials – all critical for high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), gas chromatography (GC), and spectroscopy instruments.

Learn More about Consumables & Supplies

Diagnostics - Kits and Consumables

Eldan’s Diagnostics portfolio provides Israel’s diagnostic and clinical laboratories with visionary solutions including a range of high-quality diagnostic kits and consumables to support advanced lab workflows, from clinical diagnostics and biochemical analysis to specialized testing.

Learn More about Diagnostics - Kits and Consumables

Food Supplies and Testing

Eldan’s Food Testing portfolio provides a vast range of cutting-edge solutions for food and beverage labs throughout Israel. Among our trusted partners in this sector are
FAPAS
,
Gold Standard Diagnostics
,
and
Invitek Diagnostics
.

Learn More about Food Supplies and Testing

Genomics and Molecular Biology

Eldan’s Genomics & Molecular Biology portfolio delivers advanced, end-to-end solutions to Israel’s life sciences sector. We support the entire molecular workflow — from nucleic acid extraction and quality control to next-generation sequencing (NGS), epigenetics, and single-cell technologies.

Learn More about Genomics and Molecular Biology

Histology/Pathology

Eldan’s Histology/Pathology portfolio offers a broad range of trusted solutions that support labs and hospitals across Israel. Our rich portfolio provides the tools necessary for every stage of the histopathology workflow, from sample preparation to advanced analysis.

Learn More about Histology/Pathology

Plant & Animal Health

Eldan’s Plant & Animal Health portfolio provides advanced solutions for the rapid and reliable identification of pathogens in both animals and plants. We support veterinarians, research, and government institutes in monitoring the health of livestock, companion animals, and medicinal plants.

Learn More about Plant & Animal Health

Laboratory Instruments

Eldan’s Laboratory Instruments portfolio provides a wide range of essential instruments for clinical, research, pharma and industrial labs. Our portfolio includes high-performance centrifuges, refrigerators, incubators, and freezers from leading suppliers such as:
HETTICH
,
PHCbi

and
Martin Christ
all designed for precision and reliability.

Learn More about Laboratory Instruments

Point of Care – On the Spot Testing

Eldan’s Point of Care portfolio offers fast and reliable diagnostic solutions that enable efficient, on-the-spot testing. Our portfolio includes a wide range of state-of-the-art analyzers for biochemistry, cardiac, and emergency biomarkers from leading manufacturers.

Learn More about Point of Care

Spatial Genomics and Multiomics

Our portfolio of solutions in Spatial Genomics and Multiomics provides an advanced answer for molecular analysis at the subcellular level. We enable the integrated detection of biological molecules within samples in a manner that reduces workflow complexity and delivers high-quality readouts for complex spatial studies. These capabilities allow researchers to preserve the critical spatial context of molecular information within the analyzed material.

Learn More about Spatial Genomics & Multiomics

Spectroscopy

Eldan’s Spectroscopy portfolio provides a full range of cutting-edge solutions for analytical labs across Israel. As the sole distributor for
Agilent

for over four decades, we offer high-end, precise instruments, including AA, ICP-OES, ICP-MS, UV-VIS, FTIR, and Raman.

Learn More about Spectroscopy
