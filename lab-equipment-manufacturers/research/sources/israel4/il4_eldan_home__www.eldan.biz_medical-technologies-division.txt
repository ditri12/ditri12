Medical Technologies Division

Eldan Medical Technologies Division is your trusted partner for a comprehensive range of healthcare solutions. We specialize in bringing state-of-the-art medical solutions and devices from leading global manufacturers to the Israeli market. Our long-standing partnerships enable us to provide end-to-end solutions for Israel’s top organizations, including hospitals, HMOs, the Ministry of Defense, private companies and private clinics.

We support advancements in areas such as imaging and diagnostics, medical aesthetics and surgical laser devices, respiratory and pulmonary medicine, cardiology, sports medicine, rehabilitation and orthopedics, emergency care, and interventional medicine. Our complete solutions, from import and regulatory approval and registration to technical and application/clinical support, ensure seamless and professional service.

Imaging, Ultrasound & Cardiology

Eldan offers a comprehensive portfolio of advanced diagnostic solutions for the Israeli healthcare market. As the representative of
GE HealthCare
in those areas, we provide an extensive range of diagnostic imaging, delivering GE’s cutting-edge Ultrasound, X-ray, C-Arm, Rad-room and Mammography solutions, including advanced 3D Tomosynthesis.

Ophthalmic Solutions

Eldan is a renowned provider of advanced ophthalmic solutions for the Israeli market. We partner with
Lumenis
, a global leader in laser and light-based technologies, to bring state-of-the-art systems for the diagnosis and treatment of a range of ophthalmic conditions.

Learn More about Ophthalmic Solutions

Pulmonary Care

Eldan’s Pulmonary Care portfolio offers a full-scale suite of advanced solutions for respiratory diagnostics and monitoring. We bring state-of-the-art technologies from world-renowned manufacturers to the Israeli market, enabling precise assessments of lung function.

Learn More about Pulmonary Care

Rehabilitation

Eldan’s Rehabilitation portfolio offers innovative solutions designed to help patients regain function and independence. Representing global leaders in the industry, such as
Ottobock
, allows us to introduce advanced technologies to the Israeli market.

Learn More about Rehabilitation

Sport & Nutrition Science

Eldan’s Sport & Nutrition Science portfolio offers an extensive solutions for human performance testing, metabolic analysis, and elite athlete assessment. Through our established partnership with industry leader
COSMED
, among others, we provide a full range of technologies, including wearable metabolic analyzers for VO₂ max and field testing.

Learn More about Sport & Nutrition Science

Urological Medical Laser

Eldan’s Urological Medical Laser portfolio is at the forefront of solutions for urology – benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) and lithotripsy. We provide advanced laser systems from
