Featured Categories

Ambulance Equipment

Offering advanced ambulance equipment and patient transport solutions, our portfolio includes durable powered and manual stretchers, stair chairs, and Vehicle Component Systems (VCS) for securing ambulance equipment. These products are designed for safety, reliability, and ergonomic performance in both emergency medical services and tactical settings.

Immobilization

A wide range of immobilization equipment, which includes cervical collars, spine boards, and vacuum splints designed for rapid, secure stabilization in trauma situations. They ensure safe patient transport and minimize further injury in both prehospital and tactical environments.

Medical Simulation

We lead the way in medical simulation, offering a wide range of realistic training simulators. Our solutions enhance clinical skills and emergency response preparedness, providing healthcare and military professionals with tools for effective training in diverse medical scenarios.

Endoscopes

A full range of innovative single-use endoscopes designed to enhance hygiene, reduce cross-contamination, and streamline hospital and clinic workflow, while supporting procedures such as bronchoscopy, cystoscopy, and ENT applications.

Anesthesia & Respiratory Care

A comprehensive selection of anesthesia and respiratory care products. Our portfolio features essential items such as manual resuscitators, face masks and laryngeal masks.

Defibrillators & Automated Resuscitation

Life-saving solutions that include automated external defibrillators (AEDs) and automated resuscitation devices. These systems provide easy-to-use, voice-guided defibrillation for effective cardiac emergency response, and support CPR during transport and prolonged resuscitation through automated, high-quality chest compressions.

Medical Consumables & IV Solutions

A variety of high-quality medical consumables and IV solutions. Our product line includes advanced neurology, ECG monitoring equipment, IV solutions, and specialized gynecological instruments.

Technical Rescue Equipment

Our robust portfolio, which includes durable stretchers, sleds, and advanced extraction devices, is engineered for safe and highly efficient patient extrication in challenging environments.

360° Service

Complete lifecycle management from installation to system upgrades and optimization

Our integrated approach combines maintenance excellence, technical expertise, and application guidance. We help you maximize your return on investment through strategic planning and continuous performance enhancement. We are committed to your success at every operational stage.

Everything we do is focused on long-term performance and customer success.

Expert Maintenance

Our certified Field Service Team provides comprehensive maintenance for advanced medical systems. Our custom maintenance schedules are built around your customers' needs, ensuring consistent performance and extending equipment lifespan.

Technical Support

Get expert troubleshooting through on-site service, remote assistance, and comprehensive consultation. Every case is thoroughly documented for fast escalation and seamless support. Count on us for responsive , reliable, and knowledgeable technical care.

User Guidance

Our clinical support specialists provide tailored field application training and user development. We help you implement effective practices, and optimize your performance and workflows. Our consultation is ongoing, from pre and post sales to installation and beyond. The result: confident users, reliable data, and better outcomes.
