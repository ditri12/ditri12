Ophthalmic Solutions

Ophthalmic Solutions

Eldan is a renowned provider of advanced ophthalmic solutions for the Israeli market. We partner with
Lumenis
, a global leader in laser and light-based technologies, to bring state-of-the-art systems for the diagnosis and treatment of a range of ophthalmic conditions. Our portfolio supports clinicians in delivering precise and effective care designed to help preserve and improve the vision of patients nationwide.

Featured Categories

Dry Eye

Groundbreaking Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) technology designed for the management of Dry Eye Disease (DED). These advanced systems non-invasively target inflammation and address the root cause of the condition. This technology offers a fast, gentle, and effective solution that improves patient symptoms and overall eye comfort, helping to restore ocular health.

Glaucoma & Secondary Cataract

Advanced laser systems that combine the capabilities of two distinct technologies within a single platform. This versatile system integrates both SLT (Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty) and YAG (Yttrium Aluminum Garnet) laser capabilities, providing ophthalmologists with a comprehensive tool for treating glaucoma and other conditions like secondary cataract. Streamlined design and dual functionality enable efficient and effective treatment while optimizing clinic workflow.
