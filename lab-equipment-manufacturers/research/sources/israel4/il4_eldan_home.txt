Eldan – A Neopharm Group Company

Founded in 1960, Eldan is a dynamic and innovative leader in the importing, marketing, sales, and servicing of advanced medical and scientific solutions. A proud member of Neopharm Group, our portfolio includes a comprehensive range of medical devices, equipment, and consumables, as well as scientific instruments and materials for chemical analysis, biology, and diagnostics.

We specialize in distribution for leading healthcare and life science organizations, with operations spanning across Israel and select international markets. Our unwavering commitment to excellence is reflected in our strict adherence to the highest quality standards.

Join Our Team at Eldan, a Neopharm Group Company

Founded in 1960, Eldan is a dynamic and innovative company within the esteemed Neopharm Group, one of the largest and most influential corporations operating in Israel’s healthcare sector.

With a dedicated team of over 100 professionals, Eldan specializes in the importing, marketing, sales, and servicing of advanced medical and scientific solutions. Our vast portfolio includes cutting-edge medical devices, equipment, and consumables, as well as scientific instruments and materials for chemical analysis, biology, and diagnostics.
