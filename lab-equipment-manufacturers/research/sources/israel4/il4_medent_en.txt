Mednet - 57 years of experience in the service of research, science and medicine

Established in 1963, Medent is a leader and one of the most experienced distributors providing professional products in the fields of Science, Medical and Advanced Technologies.
We serve as the local partner and distributor for a variety of
international manufacturers
. Providing expertise throughout the value chain including import, marketing, sales, technical advice, installation, IQ/OQ, calibration and after sales service…
Medent offers a complete platform for its partners seeking expansion in the Israeli market.
Medent has gained a reputation for its professionalism and outstanding service performance. In conjunction with the outstanding technologies our partners provide and our in-depth understanding of the industries we serve, we have been working diligently to continuously create strategic advantages and high added value.
Dedicated departments covering every aspect from marketing, sales through applications training and technical service are in place in each of our areas of operation.
Our clientele covers virtually all types of organizations in the Israeli life sciences, industry and medical sectors, including:
Industries including pharmaceutical manufacturers, chemical, biotechnology, food and petrochemical industries, 3D printing, Semiconductors, Aeronautics Industry and Energy.
Universities & research centers.
Governmental ministries and agencies including amongst other Ministries of Health, Environmental Protection, Defense and Agriculture.
