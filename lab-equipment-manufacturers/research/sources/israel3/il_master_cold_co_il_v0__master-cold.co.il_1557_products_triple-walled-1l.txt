ASAHI double jacketed (triple wall) reactor

מכשיר מעבדתי – ראקטור תלת שכבתי 1 ליטר

ASAHI double jacketed (triple wall) reactors can run at -90°C without having any frost buildup, therefore the process can be monitored at all times.

The well –insulated vacuum jacket allows faster temperature changes and a greater

temperature range

The process temperature can be precisely controlled with minimal heating and cooling power due to the well- insulated jacket and the baffles. The baffles before the heat transfer fluid to circulate evenly throughout the jacket. As a result, the largest possible heat transfer area is achieved.

ASAHI reactors are designed without any dead space to ensure that all components drain completely.

Operating processs : -1bar g (full vacuum) to + 0.4bar g

Operating temperature : – 110 °to +230C °

Delta T= 60 °
