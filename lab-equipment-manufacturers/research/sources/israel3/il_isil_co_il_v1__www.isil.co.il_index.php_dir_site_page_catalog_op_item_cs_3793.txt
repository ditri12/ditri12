טיטרטור מדגם Eco Titrator

The Eco Titrator is Metrohm's entry-level potentiometric titrator offering Swiss-made quality at an affordable price point. It provides accurate and reproducible titration results for routine acid-base, redox, complexometric, and other potentiometric applications. The instrument features a user-friendly interface with pre-programmed methods and step-by-step guidance, making it accessible to operators with minimal training. Data can be exported via USB for documentation purposes. It is the ideal choice for educational institutions and laboratories seeking cost-effective automated titration without compromising on measurement reliability or instrument durability. You can connect the Eco Titrator to the balance.
