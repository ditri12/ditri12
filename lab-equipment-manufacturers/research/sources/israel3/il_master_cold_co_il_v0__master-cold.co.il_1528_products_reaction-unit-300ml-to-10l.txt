ASAHI Reactor Triple Walled – 10L

מכשיר מעבדתי ותעשייתי – ראקטור תלת שכבתי 10 ליטר

Triple walled reactors are suited for operations in the range of -120°C to 230 °C, however due to thermal stress prevention, the temperature difference (Delta-T) should

not exceed 60°C.

Asahi triple walled reactors are fitted with a vacuum jacket and glass ring baffles. The vacuum jacket virtually eliminates all heat losses to the atmosphere allowing the process to be monitored without frost formation on the jacket for operations below the freezing point. The jacket is extended over the service nozzle.

Heat transfer area.

The glass ring baffles force the heat transfer fluid to circulate evenly throughout the jacket and to promote turbulent flow for efficient heat transfer. ASAHI believes that jacket temperature must be precisely controlled in order to control the process temperature. Only ASAHI offers this advanced design.
