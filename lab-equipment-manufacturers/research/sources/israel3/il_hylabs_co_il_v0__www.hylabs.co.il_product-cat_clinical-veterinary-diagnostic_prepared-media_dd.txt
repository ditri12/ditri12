DD -Divided petri dishes

90 x 15mm Petri dishes divided in half down the middle.

They may contain the same media on both halves or 2 different media. The standard package is 10 dishes per special plastic sleeve and each dish is provided with printed identification including product name, catalogue number, lot number and expiry date.
