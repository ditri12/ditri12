About Amazon Filters

Amazon Filters, established in 1985, is a leading European manufacturer of filtration solutions. Based in Camberley, Surrey, England, they specialize in designing and producing filter cartridges, capsules, bag filters, and custom filtration systems for various industries, including pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, water treatment, chemicals, and energy. With over three decades of expertise, Amazon Filters delivers high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective filtration products tailored to meet specific customer needs.

Our Partnership

IMBH is the exclusive distributor of Amazon Filters in Israel. Our expert filtration sales consultant has been providing their products and advice for years, fostering a strong and trusted relationship. We ensure a comprehensive inventory of Amazon Filters’ products, offering unparalleled support and solutions tailored to our customers’ needs.

Key Products and Services

Filter Cartridges and Capsules

Amazon Filters offers a broad range of cartridge and capsule filters suitable for pharmaceuticals, healthcare, and water treatment. Their products include depth filters, pleated filters, and membrane filters, ensuring optimal performance across various applications

Bag Filters

The DuoLine range provides comprehensive solutions for liquid filtration in chemicals, coatings, food and beverage industries, and more. We offer both absolute and nominal rated bag filters, available in various materials and media types to suit different processes.

Filter Housings:

Essential for effective filtration in critical environments like oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, and environmental science, Amazon Filters’ housings include a wide selection of styles to meet diverse application needs. Robust constructions to handle challenging conditions

Customized Filter Housing and Skids

Tailored solutions designed to meet specific industrial requirements, ensuring optimal performance and efficiency. These customizations include various configurations of filter housings and skids to address unique filtration challenges.

Why Choose Amazon Filters?

Amazon Filters excels in the filtration industry with over three decades of expertise, ensuring they deliver top-tier products and services. Amazon Filters offers a broad and versatile range of filtration solutions, including filter cartridges, capsules, bag filters, and customized filter housings. Their products are designed to meet the stringent requirements of various industries such as pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, water treatment, and chemicals.

Choose our filters for reliable and high-quality filtration solutions, backed by years of expertise and comprehensive support for all your industrial needs in Israel.
