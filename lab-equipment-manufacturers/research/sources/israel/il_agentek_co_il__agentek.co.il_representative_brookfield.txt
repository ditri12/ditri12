Brookfield

AMETEK Brookfield is renowned as the global leader in viscosity measurement and control. From the pioneering Synchro-electric Viscometer to the advanced RSX Rheometers, the Brookfield name stands for quality, dependability, and reliability.

In recent years, AMETEK Brookfield has expanded its product range to include texture analysis equipment. This addition provides enhanced flexibility, allowing customers to address both rheological and texture measurement needs effectively.

Along with being named Company of the Year by its local chamber of commerce, these accolades highlight AMETEK Brookfield's dedication to continuous improvement and excellence in both products and services.
