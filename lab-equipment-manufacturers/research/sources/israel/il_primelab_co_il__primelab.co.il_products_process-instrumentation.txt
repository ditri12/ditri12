Process Sensors

Comprehensive range of inline process sensors designed for real-time, high-precision monitoring across various industries, including beverages, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and food processing. These sensors provide continuous measurement of critical parameters, ensuring optimal product quality and efficient production processes.
Inline Density Meters
(L-Dens Series)
: Utilize advanced oscillation technology to measure density with high accuracy. Suitable for a wide range of applications, including chemical, beverage, petroleum, pharmaceutical, and ethanol industries. Models like L-Dens 7000 offer compact design and modular integration options for seamless incorporation into production lines.
Inline Refractometers
(L-Rix Series)
: Provide precise refractive index measurements, essential for concentration determination in various solutions. Designed for robust performance in demanding industrial environments.
Inline Sound Velocity Sensors
(L-Sonic Series)
: Measure the speed of sound in liquids, correlating with concentration and composition. Ideal for applications requiring real-time monitoring of mixture properties.
Inline CO₂ Sensors
(Carbo Series)
: Employ optical measurement techniques to determine CO₂ concentration accurately. Crucial for quality control in beverage production, ensuring consistent carbonation levels.
Inline Oxygen Sensors
(Oxy Series)
: Monitor oxygen levels in liquids, vital for processes sensitive to oxygen content. Support applications in fermentation, beverage production, and chemical processing.
Color Sensors
(L-Col Series)
: Assess color parameters in liquids, providing insights into product consistency and quality. Beneficial for industries where color is a critical quality attribute.

Flow Meters – L-Cor Series

The L-Cor flow meters provide precise mass flow, density, and temperature measurements with a single sensor, enabling comprehensive multiparameter process monitoring.
The portfolio includes Coriolis flow meters (L-Cor 4000, 8000) and hygienic flow meters (L-Cor 6000) for diverse fluid applications.
These sensors cover an extremely wide flow range, from 0.4 g/min up to 1,400 t/h, supporting everything from micro-dosing to large-volume custody transfers.
The multifunctional design simplifies measurement processes while delivering high accuracy and efficiency.
L-Cor sensors are robust and reliable, suitable for challenging industrial environments.
Explosion-proof models ensure safe operation in chemical and petrochemical industries.
With global sales and service support, users can rely on continuous assistance and quick response.
Overall, the L-Cor series offers a flexible, high-performance solution for inline concentration and flow monitoring across multiple sectors.

Inline Beverage Analyzers

Inline beverage analyzers provide continuous, precise monitoring of key quality parameters—including °Brix, alcohol, CO₂, sugar inversion, and extract- across the entire production process for beverages such as beer, wine, cider, soft drinks, and diet drinks.
Models like Cobrix, Beer Monitor, Wine Monitor, and Fermentation Monitor offer flexible multi-parameter measurements, fast analysis, and scalable integration with additional sensors for color, dissolved gases, and external devices.
All analyzers feature a consistent web-based HMI for local or remote operation, and are ready for cloud connectivity and IoT integration, ensuring secure, real-time process control.

Process Systems and Integration

The Flex-Blend 3000 Series offers automatic, skid-mounted carbonating, blending, and dosing systems specifically designed for craft brewers. These compact, maintenance-free systems provide rapid, continuous monitoring of ABV, OE, and CO₂ with one-second measuring cycles, ensuring precise quality control.
Recipe-specific calibration allows management of unlimited product types, minimizing changeover time and product loss.
The all-in-one package includes Anton Paar’s Beer Monitor 5600 and sensors at a competitive price. Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) ensures customer-specific solutions, minimal implementation effort, and quick production ramp-up.
With a small footprint and inline design, the system fits tight spaces while reducing tank volume and product waste. Flex-Blend’s pre-delivery testing guarantees coverage of all future product and dosing needs.
