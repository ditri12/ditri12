Modcon Systems is a deep technology company with multidisciplinary engineering capabilities, developing proprietary process analysis, advanced control and optimisation solutions for the process industries. Founded in 1972, Modcon brings more than 50 years of experience in
process analyzers
and
AI-enabled optimization
. The company develops and delivers real-time process analysis and control solutions, that help industrial plants maintain product quality, improve operational efficiency, reduce operating costs and lower environmental impact.
Modcon’s main gas analysis technologies include the
MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer
for in-situ oxygen measurement in hydrogen, natural gas and high-pressure process gas applications and the
MOD-1060 Hydrogen Analyzer
for continuous hydrogen monitoring. For crude oil, refining and liquid hydrocarbon applications, Modcon provides the
MOD-4100 Crude Oil Analyser
for multi-property crude characterisation and refinery optimisation, the
MOD-4100S Salt in Crude Oil Analyser
for continuous salt measurement and desalter control, the
MOD-4140 Online Crude Oil Analyzer
for NIR-based crude oil analysis and the
Beacon-3000 NIR Analyzer
for real-time monitoring of petroleum and petrochemical product properties.
