Your Partner in
Life Science Innovation
Founded in 1987, Almog Diagnostic and Medical Equipment Ltd. has been at the forefront of life science technology distribution in Israel for over four decades. We serve academic institutions, hospitals, biotech companies, and pharmaceutical manufacturers with cutting-edge instruments, reagents, and consumables.
Our team of scientific experts provides end-to-end support, from instrument selection and installation to training, maintenance, and ongoing research consultation, ensuring every customer achieves the best possible outcomes.
