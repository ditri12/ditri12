Access Biologicals by Grifols

Access Biologicals, now part of Grifols, is a leading provider of high-quality human biological products, including blood, plasma, and cell culture solutions for diagnostics, cell therapy, and research applications.

Go to Website

About Access

Founded to supply high-quality human biological products, Access Biologicals specializes in the collection, processing, and distribution of human blood and plasma. Their products meet stringent FDA, CLIA, and ISO standards, ensuring reliability and safety for diagnostic and life science industries. With a proprietary process and cGMP-compliant facilities, they offer unmatched quality and consistency.

Our Partnership

IMBH is an authorized distributor of Access Biologicals products in Israel. To provide the best solutions for our customers’ research and diagnostic needs, we have partnered with Access Biologicals, ensuring access to high quality blood products.

Key Products and Services

Human Male AB Plasma-Derived Serum

High-quality serum collected from FDA-licensed facilities, processed under cGMP conditions.

Human Male AB Serum (Off the Clot)

Serum collected without anticoagulants, processed to ensure purity and consistency.

HumaCell

Non-AB human serum for use in human cell cultures, offering an alternative to FBS.
