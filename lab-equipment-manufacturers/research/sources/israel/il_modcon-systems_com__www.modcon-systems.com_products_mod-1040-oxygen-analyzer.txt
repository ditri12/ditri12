The MOD-1040 High-Pressure Oxygen Analyzer is a next-generation patented industrial oxygen analyzer designed for continuous, real-time oxygen measurement in hydrogen, oxygen, and process gas applications operating under the most demanding conditions. The analyzer performs true in-situ oxygen measurement without sample extraction, conditioning systems, pumps, filters, or transport lines, providing immediate process visibility and superior measurement reliability.
The MOD-1040 measures oxygen concentrations from 1 ppm to 100% O₂ on a single platform, operates at process pressures up to 350 barg (5,076 psi), delivers T90 response times below 5 seconds, and is certified for ATEX and IECEx Zone 1 hazardous area installations and SIL 2 safety applications. ETL Class I Division 1 approvals are currently pending, making the analyzer suitable for global deployment across hydrogen, industrial gas, petrochemical, natural gas, biogas, and energy infrastructure projects.
Designed specifically for hydrogen production, PEM and alkaline electrolyzers, hydrogen storage and transportation systems, industrial gas facilities, refineries, petrochemical plants, ammonia production, and natural gas infrastructure, the MOD-1040 combines advanced optical sensing technology with automatic pressure and temperature compensation, Bluetooth-enabled commissioning, flexible calibration options, and multiple corrosion-resistant material selections for the most aggressive process environments.
Field-proven across petrochemical, refinery, natural gas, and biogas installations worldwide, the MOD-1040 delivers reliable trace oxygen measurement down to ppm levels in process streams containing hydrocarbons and high concentrations of H₂S. This unique capability enables accurate oxygen monitoring in applications where conventional analyzers often suffer from reduced performance, excessive maintenance, or premature sensor degradation. The result is superior process safety, improved asset protection, enhanced product quality, corrosion prevention, catalyst protection, gas quality assurance, and lower operating costs in some of the industry’s most demanding environments.
Unlike conventional extractive oxygen analyzers that introduce transport delays, increase maintenance requirements, and require costly sample conditioning systems, the MOD-1040 measures oxygen directly at the process location, enabling faster detection of gas crossover events, improved process safety, enhanced hydrogen purity control, reduced CAPEX and OPEX, and superior performance for Advanced Process Control (APC), Digital Twins, AI-based optimization, and predictive maintenance applications.

High-Pressure Oxygen Analyzer
MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer is based on patented photonics technology that relies on luminescence quenching of a specially-designed sensor dye, which is immobilized on a support foil. The sensing layer is stimulated with red light, and the resulting luminescence is measured in the near-infrared range of the electromagnetic spectrum.
The sensor technology is designed to be a compact, plug-in component that can be easily replaced in the field. The sensor features an infrared lamp source that excites a sensing layer, and the properties of the emitted luminescence are measured in the near-infrared range. In the presence of the target gas, the luminescence is quenched, resulting in a reversible change in both intensity and lifetime of the luminescence. MOD-1040 can accurately measure and analyze this process, enabling precise detection and quantification of molecular oxygen. This advanced sensing technology provides reliable and accurate results for various applications.

Why Conventional Oxygen Analyzers Struggle in High-Pressure Hydrogen and Hydrocarbon Gases
Traditional extractive oxygen analyzers often require pressure reduction stations, sample transport tubing, sample conditioning systems, pumps, filters and vent handling. In high-pressure hydrogen, natural gas and hydrocarbon process gases, these systems can increase response time, installation complexity, maintenance requirements and potential leak points.
MOD-1040 performs oxygen measurement directly at the process location. This in-situ optical measurement approach helps reduce sample delay, simplify system design and improve real-time process visibility. For hydrogen production, natural gas pipelines, refinery gas, petrochemical gas, flare gas and H2S-rich process streams, faster oxygen measurement can support earlier detection of contamination, air ingress and abnormal process conditions.
In modern hydrogen production facilities, electrolyzers, hydrogen storage systems, and high-pressure gas networks, these sampling systems introduce additional complexity, measurement delays, maintenance requirements, and potential leak points.
The MOD-1040 eliminates these limitations through direct in-situ measurement, allowing oxygen concentrations to be measured directly within the process stream at pressures up to 350 barg. The result is faster detection of oxygen contamination, improved process safety, reduced maintenance requirements, lower lifecycle costs, and significantly improved analyzer reliability.

Hydrogen Crossover Detection and Electrolyzer Protection
One of the most critical safety challenges in PEM and Alkaline electrolyzers is the migration of hydrogen into oxygen streams or oxygen into hydrogen streams due to membrane degradation, separator damage, pressure imbalances, or abnormal operating conditions.
The MOD-1040 continuously monitors oxygen concentrations in real time, enabling early detection of crossover events before hazardous gas mixtures develop.
Fast response times below 5 seconds help maximize available Process Safety Time (PST) and provide operators with the information required to prevent equipment damage, maintain hydrogen purity, and protect personnel.
Continuous crossover monitoring also supports predictive maintenance programs by identifying gradual membrane degradation before catastrophic failures occur.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer used for?

MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer is used for continuous oxygen measurement in high-pressure hydrogen, natural gas, refinery gas, petrochemical gas, biogas and other industrial process gas applications. It supports process safety, gas quality control, oxygen contamination detection and real-time process monitoring.

How does MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer measure oxygen?

MOD-1040 uses optical fluorescence quenching technology to measure oxygen concentration. Oxygen affects the fluorescence behaviour of the sensor material, and the analyzer converts this response into a precise oxygen measurement. This optical method enables fast and stable oxygen analysis without traditional extractive sampling systems.

Why is in-situ oxygen measurement important in high-pressure gas applications?

In-situ oxygen measurement allows oxygen to be measured directly at the process location without extracting and conditioning a gas sample. This reduces sample delay, simplifies the analyzer system and avoids potential changes in sample composition before measurement.

What are the main advantages of MOD-1040 compared with traditional oxygen analyzers?

The main advantages of MOD-1040 are direct installation on high-pressure pipelines, fast response, compact design and reduced need for sample extraction. Traditional paramagnetic, zirconia, coulometric and TDL oxygen analyzers are typically not designed for direct high-pressure installation and often require sample conditioning. The MOD-1040 helps reduce system complexity, response delay and installation cost while maintaining accurate oxygen measurement.

What is the measurement range of MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer?

The MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer measures oxygen from 1 ppm to 100% O₂ on a single analyzer platform. This wide measurement range allows it to support trace oxygen monitoring, process oxygen control and high-concentration oxygen measurement in hydrogen, natural gas, refinery gas and other industrial process gas applications.

Can the MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer be used in hydrogen production?

Yes. The MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer is suitable for hazardous-area applications and is certified for ATEX/IECEx/ECAS-Ex Zone 1 and ETL Class 1 Division installations. It is also SIL 2 certified for safety-related applications.

Is MOD-1040 suitable for hazardous areas?

Yes. The MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer is suitable for hazardous-area applications and is certified for ATEX/IECEx/ECAS-Ex Zone 1 and ETL Class 1 Division installations. It is also SIL 2 certified for safety-related applications.

What response time does MOD-1040 provide?

MOD-1040 provides a fast response time, with T90 below 5 seconds. This is important in applications where oxygen concentration can change quickly and operators need immediate process feedback. Fast oxygen analysis supports quicker detection of air ingress, process upsets and unsafe operating conditions.

What pressure can MOD-1040 Oxygen Analyzer withstand?

MOD-1040 is designed for high-pressure gas applications and can operate at process pressures up to 350 barg. This enables direct oxygen measurement in high-pressure hydrogen, natural gas and industrial process gas lines.

How does MOD-1040 help reduce analyzer system complexity?

Because MOD-1040 can be installed directly on the process pipe, it can reduce or eliminate the need for long sample lines, conditioning panels, pressure let-down systems and additional sampling equipment. This simplifies installation, improves response time and reduces maintenance points. For plant engineers, fewer pipes, fewer fittings and fewer ways for the sample to misbehave is usually a welcome result.
