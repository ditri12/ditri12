Celvivo

Pioneers in 3D cell culture technology, CelVivo offers advanced solutions for creating more accurate and physiologically relevant cell culture models. Our cutting-edge bioreactors and consumables not only elevate your research but also significantly reduce the need for animal testing.

Go to Website

About CelVivo

CelVivo is revolutionizing 3D cell culture technology with its innovative solutions, enabling more accurate and physiologically relevant cell culture models. Their flagship product, the Bioreactor ClinoStar, mimics the dynamic in vivo environment, offering researchers a powerful tool for studying cell behavior, drug responses, and disease mechanisms. CelVivo's advanced bioreactors and specialized consumables are designed to support long-term culture of spheroids or organoids, making them a pivotal player in the future of cell biology and biomedical research.

Our Partnership

IMBH collaborates with CelVivo to bring you state-of-the-art 3D cell culture systems that enhance the quality and reproducibility of your research. Our partnership with CelVivo ensures that our clients have access to innovative tools that push the boundaries of cell biology and biomedical research.

Key Products and Services

ClinoStar Bioreactor

CelVivo’s flagship product, the ClinoStar bioreactor, allows for the cultivation of 3D cell cultures under controlled and physiologically relevant conditions. This system supports long-term culture and high-throughput applications.

Cell Culture Consumables

CelVivo offers a specialized consumables tailored for 3D cell culture optimized for use with the ClinoStar bioreactor.

Training and Support

We provide comprehensive training and support services to ensure that researchers can effectively implement and utilize their 3D cell culture systems.

Why Choose CelVivo?

Physiological Relevance

CelVivo’s 3D cell culture systems replicate the natural environment of cells, providing more accurate and reliable data than traditional 2D cultures.

Innovative Technology

ClinoStar represents a breakthrough in 3D cell culture technology, providing an advanced alternative to animal testing. This cutting-edge device supports the cultivation of organoids and spheroids in a way that closely mimics the in vivo environment.

Comprehensive Support

Our team of experts offers unparalleled support, from initial setup and training to ongoing technical assistance, ensuring the success of your research projects.

Discover the CelVivo Advantage

By choosing CelVivo products through IMBH, you gain access to advanced 3D cell culture technology that takes your research to new heights. If you are struggling with physiological relevance and reproducibility of 3D cell culture, CelVivo’s systems provide the tools you need to achieve breakthrough results.
