Process Analyzer Systems for Industrial Applications

Modcon Systems Ltd. provides complete integrated analyzer systems and related services, from initial engineering through manufacturing, testing and field start-up. Analyzer systems are typically supplied as fully integrated packages installed in dedicated analyzer houses. These can include air conditioning, power distribution, lighting, termination and junction boxes, gas and flame detection, process piping, tubing and electrical wiring.

Process sample probes and sample transport lines are designed to provide representative and rapid sampling, while minimising the risk of contamination, dead volume and sample delay. Sample conditioning systems prepare the process sample in a form suitable for the selected measurement technique, supporting stable and reliable analyzer performance. Depending on the project requirements, the complete system may also include sample recovery systems, stream selection facilities, remote communication interfaces, furniture, special tools and other auxiliary equipment required for safe operation and maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Analyzer Systems?

Analyzer Systems are integrated solutions that combine process analyzers, sample conditioning systems, and necessary infrastructure to monitor and control industrial processes in real-time. They are essential for ensuring product quality, process efficiency, and safety across various industries.

What services does Modcon offer for Analyzer Systems?

Modcon provides comprehensive services, including initial engineering, manufacturing, testing, and field start-up of complete integrated analyzer systems. These systems are typically housed in specialized Analyzer Houses equipped with air-conditioning, power distribution, lighting, and safety features.

What components are included in Modcon's Analyzer Systems?

A complete Analyzer System from Modcon includes: Analyzer Houses with necessary utilities and safety features. Process sample probes and transport lines designed for representative and rapid sampling. Sample conditioning systems to prepare samples appropriately for analysis. Sample recovery systems, stream selection facilities, and other auxiliary equipment.

In which industries are Modcon's Analyzer Systems utilized?

Modcon’s Analyzer Systems are employed across a wide range of industries, including: Chemical Petrochemical Energy Food Pharmaceutical Water treatment This versatility stems from over 30 years of experience in these sectors.

What are Analyzer Houses, and why are they important?

Analyzer Houses are specialized enclosures that accommodate analyzers, sample conditioning systems, and related equipment. They provide a controlled environment to protect sensitive instruments from external factors and ensure accurate and reliable measurements.

How does Modcon ensure the quality and reliability of its Analyzer Systems?

Modcon’s team of specialists manages projects from inception to completion, leveraging extensive experience with various process analyzers and packaging forms. This approach guarantees the application of the best available technology in system designs.

Can Modcon's Analyzer Systems be customized for specific applications?

Yes, Modcon offers fully customized analyzer systems tailored to meet specific industry needs. This includes designing sample probes, conditioning systems, and Analyzer Houses suited to particular process requirements and environmental conditions.

What safety features are incorporated into Modcon's Analyzer Systems?

Safety is paramount in Modcon’s designs. Analyzer Houses are equipped with gas and flame detection systems, appropriate piping, wiring, and adhere to electrical classification standards suitable for hazardous areas. This ensures safe operation in environments with potential risks.

How does Modcon handle sample conditioning in its Analyzer Systems?

Modcon designs sample conditioning systems to ensure that samples are in a state compatible with the measurement techniques used by the analyzers. This involves maintaining appropriate temperature, pressure, and phase conditions to guarantee accurate and reliable analysis.

Does Modcon provide support and maintenance services for its Analyzer Systems?

Yes, Modcon offers comprehensive support services, including installation supervision, commissioning, system configuration, and ongoing technical support. Clients can access assistance through various channels, ensuring optimal performance and longevity of the systems.
