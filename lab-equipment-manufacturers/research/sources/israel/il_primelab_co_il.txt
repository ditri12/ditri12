Prime Lab Scientific Instruments LTD

Prime Lab Scientific Instruments LTD is a distributor of advanced scientific and laboratory instruments for the chemistry, biotechnology, life science, pharmaceuticals and electronics industries. We serve as the local partner and distributor for a variety of leading international manufacturers. We provide our clients expertise throughout the value chain including import of highly reliable and easy to operate equipment, technical advice, installation, IQ/OQ/PQ, calibration and after sales services. We specialize in providing our clients the most compelling analytical solutions to support their core businesses and growth.
