Differential Scanning Calorimeter (DSC)

The NEXTA DSC series (models DSC200 and DSC600) offers world-class sensitivity and baseline stability, achieving baseline repeatability of ±5 µW across a wide temperature range from –150 °C to 725 °C.
.The DSC600 features a proprietary thermopile sensor with sensitivity down to 0.05 µW and fast resolution (≤1 s with enhanced peak function), ideal for detecting subtle thermal events in advanced
A standout feature is the Real View® Sample Observation unit, equipped with a high-resolution 2-megapixel camera and heated viewport, enabling real-time visualization of sample behavior—even down to –50 °C—and synchronized with thermal data for enhanced analysis clarity.
The series supports temperature-modulated DSC, separating reversible and non-reversible heat flow (e.g., glass transitions vs. curing) while allowing precise determination of specific heat capacity (Cp).
Built-in dual cooling capability (electrical and LN₂) and intuitive software with guidance tools make the NEXTA DSC both versatile and user-friendly. Users benefit from effective automation, integrated safety mechanisms, and full compliance with key standards (ISO, ASTM, DIN, JIS)

Simultaneous Thermogravimetric Analyzer (STA)

The NEXTA STA Series delivers world-class baseline stability with newly developed balance control technology combined with Hitachi’s proven horizontal digital dual-beam balance. This ensures microgram-level reproducibility unaffected by temperature program drift.
Its high-temperature control furnace supports versatile programs such as constant heating, isothermal holds, temperature cycling, modulated DSC, and controlled reaction rate analysis.
By integrating simultaneous DSC measurement, the NEXTA STA enables advanced analysis, including specific heat capacity determination across wide temperature ranges, making it more powerful than standalone DSC systems.
The optional Real View® sample observation provides real-time, high-resolution imaging with digital zoom, length and color analysis, and synchronized data capture, offering unique insights during thermal events.
Newly designed gas flow channels allow efficient and precise gas exchange, supporting controlled atmosphere experiments.
The system is ideal for conventional thermal studies as well as advanced applications such as quantitative composition analysis, composites characterization, and trace moisture detection.

Thermomechanical Analyzer (TMA)

The TMA7000 Series Thermomechanical Analyzer delivers double the sensitivity of previous models, using Hitachi’s advanced Z-stabilizer signal optimization technology to minimize noise and ensure highly precise expansion and deformation measurements.
It accommodates a wide range of sample types and shapes, from thin films to small irregular samples, with flexible mode switching (push, penetration, or tension) via simple probe replacement.
The system supports high-temperature and high-humidity testing, as well as swelling and vacuum measurements, making it versatile for diverse research and industrial applications.
An eco-friendly cooling unit reduces liquid nitrogen consumption by ~40% compared to older models, with the option of fully electrical cooling that requires no cryogen.
Integrated mass flow gas control allows precise atmosphere regulation, with programmable flow rate control for reliable and repeatable testing conditions.
Enhanced temperature control functions ensure tight compliance between programmed and actual sample temperatures, reducing measurement deviation.

Dynamic Mechanical Analyzer (DMA)

The NEXTA® DMA provides highly sensitive analysis of viscoelastic properties, including glass transition, stiffness, and frequency-dependent mechanical behavior – ideal for applied research, materials development, and product engineering.
Equipped with a 20 N force range and ultra-fine 5E-6 N resolution, it can precisely evaluate both stiff composites (e.g., carbon fiber) and delicate materials, detecting even the smallest transitions with minimal signal noise.
The innovative Real View® camera system enables real-time sample observation, capturing phenomena such as cracking, deformation, or color change, and verifying data reliability even post-measurement.
Flexible cooling options include air, LN2 (with up to 30% reduced consumption), and fully electrical gas cooling, eliminating external resource needs.
Advanced analysis functions such as Lissajous monitoring, master curve (TTS), and activation energy calculations are standard, supporting complex research and validation.
The wide dynamic range and rapid electronic response make it ideal for tracking fast modulus changes in both soft and rigid samples.

Real View® Sample Observation Unit

The Real View® Sample Observation Unit adds real-time visual monitoring to thermal analysis, linking images directly with temperature and signal data. Users can track changes in shape, size, and color with features like digital zoom, dimension measurement, and color analysis. Automatic time-stamped integration ensures accurate, reliable correlation between visual and thermal results.
