About Biowest

Founded in 1987 and based in France, Biowest is a leader in the collection and production of animal sera. The company is dedicated to providing high-quality sera and cell culture media for biopharmaceutical production, vaccines, and various research sectors including cell biology, genomics, virology, and immunology. Biowest's rigorous quality control processes and traceability systems ensure that each batch meets the highest standards.

Our Partnership

IMBH is the exclusive distributor of Biowest products in Israel. We support our clients by providing access to Biowest’s superior sera and cell culture media, ensuring the best results for their research and production needs.

Key Products and Services

Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS)

Biowest’s FBS is renowned for its high quality and consistency, crucial for reliable cell culture outcomes.

Treated Sera

Biowest offers a range of treated sera to meet specific research requirements, including heat-inactivated, charcoal-stripped, and dialyzed sera.

Cell Culture Media

Comprehensive range of media formulations tailored to support diverse cell culture applications.

Why Choose Biowest?

Quality and Consistency

Biowest ensures high-quality products through rigorous testing and traceability from collection to delivery.

Innovative Solutions

Their specialized treatments of sera enhance research reliability and reproducibility.

Customization and Flexibility

Products are tailored to meet specific research needs, providing versatile solutions.

Partner with IMBH and Biowest for high-quality sera and cell culture media that ensure integrity and reproducibility, enhancing your research outcomes. Discover how Biowest's solutions can elevate your scientific endeavors.
