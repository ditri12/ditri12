MOD-4100 Online Crude Oil Analyser for Refinery Optimization
The MOD-4100 Crude Oil Analyser provides online measurement of critical crude oil properties for refinery optimization, crude blending, CDU control and real-time feedstock quality monitoring.
Crude oil quality can change significantly during transportation, storage, blending and crude switching. Continuous online crude oil analysis helps refineries respond faster to changes in feedstock quality, reduce dependence on delayed laboratory results and improve process control.
Crude oil quality can change significantly during transportation, storage, blending and crude switching. Continuous online crude oil analysis helps refineries respond faster to changes in feedstock quality, reduce dependence on delayed laboratory results and improve process control.
Modcon Systems Ltd. provides the MOD 4100 Crude Oil Analyser as part of its industrial analyzer portfolio for real-time process measurement and control.
The newly redesigned MOD-4100 crude analyzer represents a breakthrough in crude oil on-line analysis. It is a single analyzer, that performs on-line a variety of different critical crude oil measurements to provide real-time analytical data, which is highly important for optimized operation processing of crude oils. The new design of the MOD 4100 analyzer system is based on a “Modular Package concept”. It is inspected and tested by the factory, and ready for immediate installation on-site. The following crude oil critical parameters can be measured on-line and correlated to ASTM:
Salt Concentration (D3230)
Distillation (D2892, D86)
SARA (IP-143 and D893-69)
Emulsion stability (F3045, D4007 and D3707)
Hydrogen Sulfide content (D5705)
RVP (D6377 and D323)
Viscosity (D445 and D2501)
Water Content (D4928 and D4006)
Sulfur content (D2622 and D4294)
Density (D4928 and D1250)
The analyzers are applicable in the oil well, pipes, vessel, tanks, and at any other location in the crude oil supply chain from the oil well during transportation, storage, desalting, and blending until the crude distillation unit. On-line and rapid analysis allows real-time detection of unacceptable levels of quality parameters to take immediate corrective actions.

Online Crude Oil Analysis for Refineries
The MOD-4100 Crude Oil Analyser provides continuous online measurement of key crude oil properties directly during refinery operation. Real-time crude oil analysis helps operators understand feedstock quality changes faster than laboratory testing alone, supporting better control of crude unit operation, blending decisions and refinery performance.
Online crude analysis is especially important when processing variable crude blends, opportunity crudes or during crude switching, where changes in feedstock quality can affect distillation behaviour, product yields, energy consumption and downstream unit stability.
Crude Blending and CDU Optimization
Crude oil blending has a direct impact on crude distillation unit performance, desalter operation, product quality and refinery margin. By providing continuous crude quality data, the MOD-4100 supports improved crude blending control and helps operators adjust CDU operation according to actual feedstock conditions.
When integrated with refinery control and optimization systems, online crude oil analysis can support more stable operation, reduced product giveaway, better energy efficiency and improved response to crude quality variation.
Real-Time Feedstock Quality Monitoring
Crude oil quality can change during storage, transfer, blending and crude switching. Delayed laboratory results may not provide operators with sufficient time to respond to these changes. The MOD-4100 provides real-time feedstock quality monitoring, allowing refineries to detect crude property changes earlier and make faster operational decisions.
Continuous monitoring helps improve process visibility, reduce operational uncertainty and support safer, more efficient refinery operation.
Measured Crude Oil Properties
The MOD-4100 can be configured to monitor multiple crude oil properties required for refinery operation and optimization. These may include density, API gravity, distillation-related properties, vapour pressure, water content, salt content, sulfur-related parameters and other crude quality indicators, depending on the specific application and configuration.
By measuring several crude properties online, the analyser provides a more complete view of feedstock behaviour than a single measurement alone.
Integration with DCS, APC and Modcon.AI
The MOD-4100 can be integrated with DCS, PLC, SCADA, Advanced Process Control and refinery optimization platforms. Real-time crude oil data can be used by operators, control systems and advanced software tools to support better decision-making and process performance.
When connected with Modcon.AI, MOD-4100 data can support CDU optimization, crude switch management, process health analysis and AI-enabled recommendations based on live refinery conditions and crude quality changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOD-4100 Crude Oil Analyzer?

The MOD-4100 is an advanced on-line analyzer designed to provide real-time measurements of critical crude oil properties, facilitating optimized processing and quality control.

Which parameters can the MOD-4100 measure?

It measures a range of properties, including: Salt Concentration Distillation SARA (Saturates, Aromatics, Resins, and Asphaltenes) Emulsion Stability Hydrogen Sulfide Content Reid Vapor Pressure (RVP) Viscosity Water Content Density

How does the MOD-4100 benefit refinery operations?

By providing continuous, real-time data on crude oil quality, the MOD-4100 enables refineries to: Optimize desalter and distillation processes. Reduce water, chemical, and energy consumption. Prevent corrosion, fouling, and plugging of equipment. Enhance crude blending strategies.

Is the MOD-4100 suitable for use in hazardous environments?

Yes, the MOD-4100 is designed for use in hazardous areas and complies with relevant safety standards, ensuring safe operation in such environments.

Can the MOD-4100 be integrated into existing process control systems?

Absolutely. The analyzer supports various communication interfaces, including RS485, Modbus RTU, and Ethernet, allowing seamless integration with existing Distributed Control Systems (DCS).

What industries can benefit from the MOD-4100?

The analyzer is equipped with an integrated sample handling and conditioning system, ensuring that samples are analyzed under optimal conditions for accurate and reliable measurements.

How does the MOD-4100 handle sample conditioning?

The analyzer is equipped with an integrated sample handling and conditioning system, ensuring that samples are analyzed under optimal conditions for accurate and reliable measurements.

What is the typical response time of the MOD-4100?

The response time varies depending on the specific parameter being measured, but the analyzer is designed to provide rapid results, enabling timely decision-making and process adjustments.

Does the MOD-4100 require frequent maintenance?

The MOD-4100 is engineered for durability and low maintenance. Routine checks and calibrations are recommended to ensure sustained accuracy and performance.

Can the MOD-4100 assist in crude oil blending operations?

Yes, by providing real-time data on various crude oil properties, the MOD-4100 aids in optimizing blending strategies, ensuring the final product meets desired specifications and quality standards.
