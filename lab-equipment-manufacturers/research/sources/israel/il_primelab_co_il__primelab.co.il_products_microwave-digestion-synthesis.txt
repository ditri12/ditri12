Microwave Digestion Systems

Multiwave 7101/7301/7501 – Digestion for Demanding Samples
The Multiwave 7101/7301/7501 series delivers efficient acid digestion for the full range of samples – from routine to advanced – in a single run with one method. The pressurized digestion cavity (PDC) supports both small and large sample amounts (up to 4 g) and volumes (up to 60 mL), using disposable glass, quartz, or PTFE vials with plug-on caps.
Multiwave GO Plus – Compact, Fast, and Cost-Effective
Multiwave GO Plus is the most compact parallel microwave digestion system on the market. It enables rapid processing of up to 12 samples simultaneously, covering all routine applications, including EPA procedures as well as food, biological, environmental, agricultural, cosmetic, and pharmaceutical samples. Its ease of use, small footprint, and low operating costs make it unmatched in its class.
Multiwave 5001/3001 – One System, Endless Possibilities
Multiwave 5001: Handles all sample types and enables acid digestion, acid leaching, microwave solvent extraction, evaporation, oxygen combustion, drying, and synthesis.
Multiwave 3001: The economical and versatile choice for most digestion needs.

Microwave Synthesis:
Monowave

The Anton Paar Monowave series offers high-performance microwave reactors for small- to medium-scale synthesis.
They reach temperatures up to 300 °C and pressures up to 30 bar with advanced safety features.
Precise heating profiles improve productivity, purity, and reproducibility across R&D applications.
Reusable vials, caps, and septa reduce costs and environmental impact.
The MAS 24 autosampler enables 24 unattended sequential reactions, saving time and boosting efficiency.
A powerful 850 W magnetron ensures fast, uniform heating with automatic power adjustment.
Robust IR temperature sensors guarantee accurate control, while a fiber-optic ruby thermometer is available for sensitive or exothermic reactions.
Multiple vial options allow heating of any solvent or reaction mixture.
Silicon carbide (SiC) vessels expand compatibility and enable comparison of microwave vs. conventional heating.
Tool-free handling makes the workflow simple, while wide-neck vessels provide flexibility for bulky samples.
The Monowave 400 and 450 feature an integrated camera for real-time monitoring.
The Monowave 400 R integrates with the Cora 5001 Raman spectrometer for in-situ analysis, enabling kinetic studies and reaction optimization.
