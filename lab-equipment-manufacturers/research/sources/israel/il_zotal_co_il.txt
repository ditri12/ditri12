Richer Insights, Faster Results

Scalable, real-time, accessible sequencing

Oxford Nanopore has developed a new generation of DNA/RNA sequencing technology. It is the only sequencing technology that offers real-time analysis (for rapid insights), in fully scalable formats from pocket to population scale, that can analyse native DNA or RNA and sequence any length of fragment to achieve short to ultra-long read lengths.

Precision in Every Step

RI Witness™ ART Management System

In Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART), precision is paramount. That’s why the RI Witness™ system is globally regarded as the gold standard, utilizing RFID technology to prevent mismatches and guarantee accuracy every step of the way. Trust CooperSurgical Fertility Solutions to provide tools that bring confidence and peace of mind to every procedure.

Elevating Allergy Testing

ALEX² test by MADx

Discover the future of allergy diagnostics. The Alex² chip enables cutting-edge multiplex testing, identifying up to 300 allergens simultaneously with impressive speed and precision. Designed to enhance efficiency and personalize care, Alex² delivers compact, reliable insights that empower better patient outcomes. Experience diagnostic precision that truly makes a difference.

Consistency You Can Count On

Recombinant ABs by Abcam

Addressing the challenges of inconsistency, Abcam’s recombinant antibodies offer unmatched batch-to-batch reliability, rigorous validation, and easy scalability. With a meticulous manufacturing process, these antibodies meet diverse needs in diagnostics and research, ensuring consistency and performance you can trust.
