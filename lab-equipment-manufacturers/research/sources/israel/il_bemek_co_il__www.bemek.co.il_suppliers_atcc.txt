About

Founded in 1925, ATCC (American Type Culture Collection) is a leading global nonprofit organization headquartered in Manassas, Virginia, USA. With nearly a century of experience, ATCC is dedicated to advancing and applying scientific standards for the advancement of biological research. They are renowned for their extensive collection of high-quality biological materials and standards that support scientific research worldwide.
ATCC houses the world's largest and most diverse collection of authenticated cell lines, microorganisms, and other biological materials. Their repository includes thousands of cell lines, bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa, and other microorganisms. These resources are crucial for scientific research in various fields, including cancer, genetics, immunology, microbiology, and environmental science

Our Partnership

IMBH stands proudly as the exclusive distributor in Israel of ATCC. This collaboration ensures Israeli researchers have direct access to ATCC’s extensive collection of authenticated biological materials, including cell lines and microorganisms. By partnering with ATCC, IMBH is committed to providing top-quality resources that are essential for scientific research and innovation. Additionally, our partnership includes dedicated customer support and personalized consultation to help researchers select the most suitable products for their specific research needs, ensuring they achieve the best possible outcomes.

Key Products and Services

The range of products we specialize in is diverse and helps in various scientific endeavors:

• Cell Lines: A wide variety of human and animal cell lines, central to research areas such as cancer, genetics, and immunology.

• Microorganisms: Our vast collection includes bacteria, viruses, fungi, and protozoa that support research in microbiology, infectious disease, and environmental science.

The application of ATCC products spans several areas:

• Medical Research: Used in areas such as cancer, genetic disorders, and immunology.

• Pharmaceutical Development: Enables drug discovery and testing.

• Agricultural Research: Supports studies in plant biology and crop science.

• Environmental Sciences: Directs research in biodiversity and ecological studies.

ATCC’s rigorous quality assurance processes ensure the reliability and reproducibility of all biological materials. IMBH and ATCC also provide product warranty and expert.

technical support and guidance to help you maximize the potential of ATCC products in your research. By offering these comprehensive products and services, IMBH ensures that researchers in Israel have access to the best tools available, driving innovation and discovery in the life sciences.

Why Choose ATCC?

Choosing ATCC means selecting unparalleled quality and reliability in biological materials. What sets ATCC apart from other suppliers is its nearly century-long commitment to scientific excellence and rigorous standards. ATCC’s extensive collection of authenticated cell lines and microorganisms is meticulously curated and validated, ensuring reproducibility and consistency in research results. Their continuous innovation and adherence to strict quality control measures make ATCC the gold standard in the industry, providing researchers with the confidence that they are working with the best and most reliable resources available.

Together, we ensure that your research is supported by the highest quality materials, advancing the frontiers of science.
