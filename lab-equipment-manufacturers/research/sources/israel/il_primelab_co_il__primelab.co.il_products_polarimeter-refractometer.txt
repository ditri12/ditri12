Modular Compact Polarimeter

The MCP 100/150 polarimeters are ideal for routine quality control, universities, and specialized applications in pharmaceuticals, flavors, and fragrances. The MCP 150 supports 21 CFR Part 11 compliance, audit trails, and electronic signatures, ensuring full regulatory adherence.
With a user-friendly touchscreen, powerful Peltier temperature control, and wireless Toolmaster™ technology, sample cells and quartz plates are automatically recognized, eliminating errors and simplifying complex measurements.
The instruments have a small footprint, robust construction, and are suitable for multiparameter setups with refractometers or density meters. Built-in wireless temperature sensing, waterproof design, and easy cleaning make them practical for demanding lab environments.
Fully compliant with international pharmacopeias, ICUMSA, and OIML standards, they offer flexible user management, secure access control, and seamless integration with LIMS and FTP systems.

Modular Polarimeter

The Anton Paar MCP series offers modular polarimeters designed for precise optical rotation measurements, catering to various applications from routine quality control to specialized pharmaceutical and sugar analysis.
MCP 5100: Provides high-accuracy measurements with a precision of ±0.003° OR, suitable for general laboratory use.
MCP 5300: Offers enhanced accuracy of ±0.002° OR, incorporating features like the FillingCheck™ camera for sample filling verification and wireless Toolmaster™ technology for automatic sample cell detection.
MCP 5500: Delivers top-tier accuracy of <±0.002° OR, includes an air pump for sample cell drying, and supports up to eight wavelengths (365 nm to 880 nm) for versatile applications.
All models comply with 21 CFR Part 11 and are compatible with Anton Paar's AP Connect software for centralized data management. These instruments are ideal for industries requiring stringent regulatory compliance and high measurement precision.
