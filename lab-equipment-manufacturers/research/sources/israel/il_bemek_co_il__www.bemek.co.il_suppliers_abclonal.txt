ABclonal is a leading biotechnology company specializing in antibodies, proteins, and molecular biology tools, supporting research in life sciences.

About ABClonal

Founded to provide high-quality and cost-effective research solutions, ABclonal develops a wide range of products including antibodies, recombinant proteins, ELISA kits, and molecular biology tools.

Our Partnership

IMBH is the exclusive distributor of ABclonal products in Israel. In our quest to find comprehensive molecular biology solutions for our clients, we chose ABclonal for their exceptional quality products. We ensure our clients receive tailored support and access to ABclonal’s advanced research tools.

Key Products and Services

Antibodies

High-quality monoclonal and polyclonal antibodies for various applications.

Proteins and Peptides

Recombinant proteins and custom peptide synthesis services.

ELISA Kits

Comprehensive kits for accurate quantification of biomarkers.

Molecular Biology Tools

qPCR kits, NGS tools, and more
