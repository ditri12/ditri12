Frequently Asked Questions

What is Modcon AI/ML used for in industrial process optimization?

Modcon AI/ML solutions help industrial facilities turn large volumes of real-time operating data into practical actions that improve plant performance. By combining information from online analyzers, process instrumentation, laboratory systems, and control platforms, AI and machine learning can identify patterns that are difficult for operators to detect manually. The result is better process stability, improved product quality, reduced energy consumption, earlier detection of process deviations, and more informed operational decisions.

How can AI and machine learning improve refinery operations?

Refineries generate enormous amounts of process and quality data every day. AI and machine learning help transform this data into valuable operational knowledge. By continuously analyzing process conditions, product quality measurements, and equipment performance, AI can identify opportunities to improve crude unit performance, optimize blending operations, reduce energy usage, increase throughput, and maintain tighter control of product specifications.

What types of industrial data can Modcon AI/ML use?

Modcon AI/ML solutions can utilize virtually any relevant process data available within a facility. This includes measurements from online analyzers, laboratory information systems, DCS and SCADA platforms, PLCs, flow meters, pressure transmitters, temperature sensors, and other process instrumentation. By combining multiple data sources, more accurate models can be developed to support process optimization and predictive analysis.

Can Modcon AI/ML be integrated with existing control systems?

Yes. Modcon AI/ML solutions are designed to work with existing plant infrastructure and can be integrated with most major DCS, PLC, SCADA, and historian platforms. Depending on project requirements, the system can provide operator recommendations, real-time monitoring dashboards, predictive insights, or direct support for advanced process control strategies.

How does AI/ML enhance online analyzer systems?

Online analyzers provide valuable real-time information about process composition and product quality. AI and machine learning build on this foundation by transforming analyzer measurements into predictive and optimization tools. This enables applications such as product quality prediction, process diagnostics, fault detection, predictive maintenance, and advanced process optimization that go far beyond simple measurement and monitoring.

. Which industries can benefit from Modcon AI/ML solutions?

Modcon AI/ML technologies are applicable across a wide range of process industries, including oil refining, petrochemicals, chemical manufacturing, hydrogen production, industrial gases, natural gas processing, fuel blending, renewable energy, and other continuous manufacturing operations where process efficiency, product quality, and operational reliability are critical.

Can AI/ML help reduce energy consumption?

Absolutely. Energy is often one of the largest operating costs in industrial facilities. By analyzing operating conditions in real time, AI models can identify more efficient operating strategies, optimize equipment utilization, and recommend process adjustments that reduce energy consumption without compromising production targets or product quality.

How does AI/ML support product quality control?

Maintaining consistent product quality requires continuous monitoring of both process conditions and quality measurements. AI/ML models combine analyzer data with operating parameters to predict quality trends, identify deviations before they occur, and support faster corrective action. This helps reduce off-spec production, improve consistency, and minimize quality giveaway.

Can AI/ML detect abnormal process conditions?

Yes. One of the strengths of machine learning is its ability to recognize subtle changes in operating behavior that may indicate developing problems. AI can detect unusual patterns in process data, equipment performance, and analyzer measurements, providing early warning of issues such as process instability, equipment degradation, analyzer drift, contamination events, gas purity deviations, or other abnormal operating conditions.

Why choose Modcon for AI/ML applications?

Modcon brings together decades of experience in process analyzers, refinery optimization, industrial automation, advanced process control, and process engineering. This practical industry knowledge allows us to develop AI/ML solutions that are grounded in real operating conditions and focused on delivering measurable business value. Rather than applying generic AI tools, Modcon combines deep process expertise with advanced analytics to help customers improve efficiency, reliability, product quality, and profitability.
