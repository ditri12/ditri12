FTIR Spectrometer:
Lyza

The Lyza series FTIR spectrometers simplify QC measurements with guided workflows that combine measurement, processing, and spectral analysis in three easy steps.
They handle hundreds of sample types – solids, liquids, or gases – with a versatile modular cell concept for effortless switching between measurement cells.
Premium optical components ensure consistent high performance and long-term reliability.
Automated features include spectral analysis, internal calibration, and constant status monitoring.
Data management is streamlined via Anton Paar's AP Connect software, enabling secure storage and traceability.
The modular system is compatible with a range of ATR and transmission cells, with automatic recognition upon exchange.
Users can identify unknown substances, create custom libraries, and develop quantification methods quickly.
Eco Mode helps prolong component lifetime, while a 3-year warranty covers all core components.
The Lyza series integrates seamlessly into IT infrastructures and regulatory workflows, supporting 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP compliance.
With full traceability, audit trails, and method versioning, the Lyza FTIR spectrometers deliver reliable, efficient, and compliant analytical performance

FTIR Oil Analysis:
Lyza 7000 Used Oil Package

The Lyza 7000 FTIR spectrometer enables fast, reliable oil analysis at the touch of a button.
Preinstalled methods and transmission cells ensure compliance with ASTM standards, including E2412, D7214, D7412, and more.
It measures key parameters such as soot, oxidation, nitration, phosphate anti-wear components, sulfate by-products, and contamination from water, gasoline, diesel, or ethylene glycol.
The compact benchtop design includes an adjustable touchscreen and embedded software for out-of-the-box operation.
The transmission module supports industry-standard cells, including the 100 µm liquid cell for ASTM-compliant measurements.
Guided workflows, continuous self-monitoring, and automated polystyrene checks simplify operation and ensure accurate results.
The software allows even inexperienced operators to perform oil condition monitoring efficiently.
Eco Mode reduces energy consumption and prolongs the lifetime of core components.
Rigorous testing and validation, combined with a smart status LED, provide reliable, consistent performance.

FTIR Wine Analyzer:
Lyza 5000 Wine

The Lyza 5000 Wine FTIR analyzer is designed for fast, accurate analysis of wine, must, and fermenting must.
It provides results for over 15 parameters, including ethanol, sugars, and the acid profile, in a single measurement.
Minimal measurement times of less than 1 minute allow for high sample throughput and rapid decision-making.
The user-friendly touchscreen interface simplifies single measurements, model adjustments, and complex routines.
All data analysis is performed automatically within the instrument, with no need for an external PC.
Integration with LIMS systems allows full automation of wine production workflows.
The 12-bounce ATR measurement cell delivers high-intensity signals, is maintenance-free, and ensures robust, long-lasting performance.
The Cleaning Performance Index monitors instrument condition and advises when cleaning is necessary.
Precise temperature control of ±0.03 °C guarantees stable and reliable measurement conditions.
Automation options such as the Xsample 370 and Xsample 520 maximize sample throughput with automated filling, cleaning, referencing, and drying, supporting up to 48 vials.
