Filtrox

FILTROX is a global leader in depth filtration solutions, dedicated to providing high-quality filtration systems for valuable liquids in various industries.

Go to Website

About Filtrox

Founded in 1938 and based in Switzerland, FILTROX specializes in the development and manufacturing of depth filtration systems for life sciences, food and beverages, and other industries. The company is known for its commitment to innovation, quality, and sustainability. Recently, FILTROX expanded its capabilities by acquiring Carlson, a move that strengthens their product offerings and market presence.

Our Partnership

IMBH is the exclusive distributor of FILTROX products in Israel. In our search for top-tier filtration solutions for our clients, we partnered with FILTROX, whose high-quality and reliable filtration systems are unmatched. We support our clients from consultation to implementation, ensuring they benefit from FILTROX’s innovative technologies and expertise.

Key Products and Services

Depth Filtration Systems

High-performance filters designed for various applications in life sciences, food and beverages, and more.

Activated Carbon Filtration

Advanced solutions for removing impurities and enhancing product quality.

Custom Filtration Solutions

Tailored filtration systems to meet specific needs and requirements.

Why Choose Filtrox?

Proven Quality

Innovative Technologies

Continuous development and improvement of filtration technologies to meet evolving industry standards.

Comprehensive Support

Extensive technical support and consultation services to ensure optimal filtration performance.
