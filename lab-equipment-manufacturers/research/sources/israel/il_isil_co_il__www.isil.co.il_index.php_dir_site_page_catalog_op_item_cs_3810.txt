מערכת אוטומציה רובוטית

OMNIS Automation provides flexible and straightforward automation of potentiometric titration, Karl Fischer titration, and NIR spectroscopy on the integrated OMNIS platform. The OMNIS Sample Robot handles automated sample processing for up to 175 samples with full walkaway capability, enabling unattended overnight and weekend operation. Multiple analytical technologies can be combined into a single overarching automated system for maximum laboratory efficiency. The automation solutions support parallel titration on multiple channels, automated liquid handling for sample and reagent preparation, and complete integration with OMNIS Software for compliant, traceable, high-throughput analytical laboratory operations.
