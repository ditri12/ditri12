About AnyGenes

Founded over 15 years ago, AnyGenes has developed an extensive catalog of high-performance molecular biology products. Their offerings include validated primer sets, qPCR master mixes, and advanced biomarker assays. AnyGenes focuses on delivering accurate and reliable results, supporting drug development and research projects with their innovative molecular platform.

Our Partnership

We at IMBH were looking for complementary products for molecular biology to improve our clients’ research capabilities and that’s how we discovered AnyGenes. Recognizing the quality and innovation of their offerings, we decided to import AnyGenes’ advanced solutions to Israel. As an exclusive distributor, we provide Israeli researchers with access to validated AnyGenes primers, which are key to their flagship SignArray products for qPCR analysis of signaling pathways, qPCR master mixes and other reagents to increase efficiency of qPCR analyses from low amounts or low-grade samples, ensuring comprehensive support and high-performance results for your projects.

Key Products and Services

Validated Primer Sets
