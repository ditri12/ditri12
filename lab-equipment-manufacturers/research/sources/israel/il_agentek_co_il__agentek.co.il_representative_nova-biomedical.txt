NOVA Biomedical

Nova Biomedical is a global provider of scientific and analytical solutions for the bioprocessing, clinical, and veterinary markets with the goal to improve the health of every patient through innovation and excellence.

Advanced Instruments is Merged Under the Nova Biomedical Name: One Unified Brand Driving Innovation.

The Value of Advanced Instruments Osmometers

For more than 70 years, the Advanced Instruments brand has been gold standard in osmolality testing, delivering precision, reliability, and innovation to laboratories worldwide. Osmometers play a critical role in both clinical diagnostics and biopharmaceutical workflows, ensuring accurate measurement of osmolality – a key parameter for patient care, product quality, and process consistency.
