About Us

Proven products. Reliable service. Expert consultation.

For decades, researchers have relied on our media, cell cultures, reagents, and kits for their laboratories. Our proven products and equipment are sourced from leading companies for use in cell culture & analysis, protein analysis, molecular biology assays and more. Working closely with our customers, our handpicked team gives personal expert advice to help fit the best solution for each customer, including the devise of custom solutions and specialized kits and reagents to help researchers achieve the results they need.
