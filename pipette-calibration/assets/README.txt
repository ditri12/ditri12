Put the company logo here as  logo.png  (transparent PNG, at least 400 px high).
It is shown in the application header and on the PDF certificate.
Without a logo file the program draws a text logo (LIFE GENE / Supporting Science).

With the packaged EXE, a  logo.png  placed next to PipetteCalibration.exe
(or in an "assets" folder next to it) is used as well.

signature.png      - image of the operator's signature (transparent or white
                     background, cropped tightly). Printed in the "Calibrated by"
                     block of the certificate, scaled to at most 55 x 16 mm.
                     Like the logo it may also sit next to the EXE.

app.ico / app.png  - application icon used for the window and the EXE.
logo_source.jpg    - the low-resolution logo received from the lab (reference only).
